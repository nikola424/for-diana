D1: You’re Losing Her Respect Every Time You Say “Okay”

Preview Free: the 3 things I’d do instead

Why keeping the peace makes the fights worse

Most married men I coach keep the peace the same way. She says something they don’t agree with, and they say “okay.”

And every complaint you swallow doesn’t disappear. It comes back later, as a fight about everything.

So here’s what I’d do instead, in 3 simple steps:

Notice the no

Say it the first time

Stay calm when she pushes back

1: Notice the no

Before you answer her, ask yourself one thing: do I actually agree? If the answer is no, that’s the moment that matters.

2: Say it the first time

“I don’t want to do that.” Not three days later, and not in a text. Right then, in a normal voice.

3: Stay calm when she pushes back

She might be surprised. That’s okay. You don’t need to win the argument. You need her to see you mean it.

Notice the no. Say it the first time. Stay calm when she pushes back.

D4: DON’T Get Louder

Preview Why being firm has nothing to do with volume

Leadership is not dominance

When a man finally decides to stop asking permission, he usually goes too far the other way. He gets loud, cold, or he starts “putting his foot down.”

That’s not leading. That’s the same fear, just louder.

You can’t lead someone you’re afraid of. And she can’t lean on a man who only speaks up when he’s angry.

The men I coach who change their marriage fastest are the calmest ones in the room. They say what they want once, and they don’t take it back.

This isn’t for the man who wants to win every argument.

It’s for the man who wants his wife to feel safe following him.

D5: Turn “We Need To Talk” Into A Plan [COPY ME]

Preview Free: the 3-part way I set up a hard conversation

How to bring something up without it turning into an attack

Part 1: The opener. “There’s something that’s been bothering me, and I want to talk about it calmly.”

Part 2: The one thing. Say what happened and how it felt, in two sentences. Not a list of everything from the last year.

Part 3: The ask. “Next time, I’d like us to…” Then stop, and let her answer.

Copy it, change the words to fit your marriage, and use it this week.

D6: The Most EFFECTIVE Way To Stop Fights From Spiraling

Preview Hint: it’s not staying quiet

3 ways to keep a fight from turning into old history

1. Talk about today. The second you bring up what happened two years ago, she will too.

2. Slow down. When your voice gets faster, hers does too. Say less, and say it slower.

3. End with a next step. “So next time, we’ll…” A fight that ends with a plan doesn’t need to happen again.
