The VSL script

~9 min recorded by Diana 13 boxes

01

Why you’re here, and why it isn’t enough

You’re here right now because you came across my content. The role plays, the arguments you’ve probably had in your own kitchen, the videos about why you need to stop asking for her permission.

And the truth is, watching videos can only get you so far. If you know exactly what you should have said, but you still freeze the second she’s upset, it’s useless. If you understand the pattern but nothing changes in how you show up, you’ll keep having the same fight.

02

The promise, and the first proof

Over the past few years I’ve coached over a hundred married men out of this exact pattern.

So if you stick with me through this short video, I’m going to show you exactly how to save your marriage without couples therapy, in a way nobody has properly explained to you yet.

Like one client of mine, who told me: “over the years of being married I got this delusion that I needed to be someone else.” Thirty days later he said: “I can be myself, and sometimes that makes her angry, and that’s okay.” And look, I’ve got plenty more men who’d tell you the same.

03

What this isn’t

But right off the bat, I want you to know this has nothing to do with sitting on a couch talking about your childhood for a year. It’s nothing like the videos that tell you to stop caring about her feelings. And it’s not about buying flowers, doing more chores, or saying sorry until she calms down.

04

Name it, and answer “too late”

So what’s this actually called? It’s assertive communication, practised through role play. We rehearse the exact conversations you’ve been avoiding until saying them feels normal. And don’t worry, it’s not too late. Your marriage isn’t broken. You just weren’t shown how.

05

Her story, short

I’m going to walk you through, start to finish, how this works. But first, you should know something about me. I used to be this woman. The scorekeeping, the “after everything I do,” dragging up the past the second someone called me out. I told myself I just had high standards.

I didn’t. I was miserable, and I was making someone else carry it. And here’s what I never told anyone: I didn’t respect the men who just took it. So it doesn’t matter how long it’s been this way in your house.

06

Why now

And I’ll be the one to tell you, you’re watching this at a perfect time. Every complaint you swallow doesn’t disappear. It just waits for a worse moment to come out. A small frustration raised on a calm Tuesday is a five-minute conversation. The same frustration held for six months comes out in the middle of a fight about something else. The longer you wait, the bigger it gets.

07

Who this is for

This is for you if you run a business and lead people all day, then walk through your front door and feel like you need permission. If you keep the peace by saying yes, then resent it later. If you love your wife and want her to feel safe with you again, not managed by you.

08

The three things I’ll prove

In the next few minutes I’m going to prove three things to you. One, why the way you’re handling it now is making it worse. Two, exactly how role play changes what you do in the moment. And three, why you don’t need her to change first.

09

Why it’s getting worse

When every answer you give your wife is yes, she loses the ability to trust any of them. She can’t tell the difference between what you want and what you’re putting up with.

And every time you fold, you confirm what she secretly fears: that nobody is steady enough to hold the line with her. You can’t lead someone you’re afraid of.

10

How it works, through one husband

Let me walk you through what this looks like for one man. His wife corrects him in front of her parents. Before, he’d go quiet, then bring it up three weeks later in the middle of a fight about the dishes.

In our sessions, I play his wife. I say what she says, the way she says it. He practises staying calm, picking his moment, and saying it plainly: “When you corrected me in front of your parents last night, I felt disrespected.” We run it until it feels normal.

Then he does it at home. She pushes back, the way we practised. He stays calm and comes back to his point. That’s the whole change. And because he’s already said it ten times with me, it comes out calm instead of angry.

11

Why you can do it, and what I got wrong

You don’t need to be a smooth talker. You need practice, the same way you practised everything else you’re good at.

When I started coaching I got plenty wrong. I gave men advice and sent them home, and they froze the second she raised her voice. Advice alone isn’t enough. That’s why everything I do now is role play, so you’ve already had the conversation before you have it for real.

12

What you get when you work with me

When you work with me, you get one-on-one role-play coaching with me playing your wife, the exact words for the conversations you’ve been putting off, and a plan for the moments that keep turning into fights. We work on who you are in those moments, not just what you say, so the change actually sticks.

13

The men, and the next step

Over a hundred married men have already made this shift. Their fights got shorter and stopped spiralling into old history. They learned to bring things up without it turning into an attack. Their wives finally understood what had been bothering them.

If that sounds like what you want for your marriage, tap the button below and see if you qualify. You don’t need to try harder. You need to practise, and I’ll practise with you.
